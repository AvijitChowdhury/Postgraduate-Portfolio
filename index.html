01 — About

An interdisciplinary research profile

I hold a B.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering from the Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET), with research spanning signal processing, deep learning, robotics, and embedded systems. My undergraduate thesis, Freq-AFFNet, developed a frequency-time-aware deep learning architecture for induction motor fault diagnosis using acoustic mel-spectrograms — now submitted to a Q1 journal.

As a Research Assistant in the Dept. of Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering, I optimize lightweight deep learning models for satellite image segmentation. Across 14 peer-reviewed publications and 38 citations, my work bridges mechanical systems, computer vision, and electrical/embedded engineering — from engine knock detection and induction-motor diagnostics to blockchain security and retrieval-augmented LLMs. I've also reviewed 17+ manuscripts for Elsevier and Springer journals.

My research goal is to develop physics-informed and data-driven methods for intelligent mechanical systems — predictive maintenance, structural health monitoring, and cyber-physical autonomy — which is why I'm applying broadly to Ph.D. programs across Mechanical Engineering, Computer Science, and Electrical & Computer Engineering.
